Setup now starts with a simple choice: SolisCloud only, Local stick only, or Local stick with SolisCloud backup. The local stick form shows only the logger type, address and inverter serial; port, slave, logger serial and gateway model moved to a collapsed Advanced section, and the web password is asked only when the stick rejects the factory one. Reconfigure is a menu (SolisCloud account, Plants, Local stick, Connection), and a local entry can add SolisCloud as a backup there. The options dialog has a timezone dropdown (Automatic, Plant offset from Solis, or a typed zone) with the request limits under Advanced, and saving reloads the entry. New Solarman sticks are discovered over DHCP (MAC prefixes E8:FD:F8 and BC:54:F9) and offered with a confirm step. Web page sticks that serve `status.html` (made by IGEN/Solarman for Solis) are supported, and a rejected stick password now asks for the new one through reauthentication. New sensors: Grid import total, Grid export total and Load total from SolisCloud; they take over the history of solis-sensor's Total Energy Purchased, Total On-grid Energy and Total Energy Used. Hybrid inverters read locally now report AC power, temperature, frequency and status. Local entries no longer create an empty "Local" plant device, the logger device carries the stick's MAC, phase sensors of single-phase inverters are disabled by default, lifetime energy shows one decimal, and battery power and the alarm have direction and state icons. A rejected API key, a SolisCloud outage and abnormal cloud data no longer open repairs (reauthentication or a log line instead), no repair is fixable any more, several repairs link to the troubleshooting page, and repairs stay open across a reload. The documentation was rewritten with a connection guide per stick model, the grid and battery sign conventions for the Energy dashboard, and new example automations. The upscaled @2x brand images were removed.
